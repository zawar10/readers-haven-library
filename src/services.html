Digital Services

Readers' Haven goes beyond printed books. Search our collection online, borrow e-books, book a quiet study room, print your notes, and stay connected with free Wi-Fi.

What We Offer

🔍

Online Catalogue

Search our full collection of books, magazines and reference material from home or inside the library, and check if a title is available.

How to use it

  • Open the catalogue on the library computers.
  • Search by title, author or subject.
  • Note the shelf number and ask the desk if you need help.

Available: 24 hours a day

📱

E-books

Read popular novels, textbooks and study guides on your phone, tablet or laptop. Members can borrow digital titles without visiting the library.

How to use it

  • Ask the front desk for your member login.
  • Sign in and choose an e-book.
  • Tap Borrow and start reading.

Available: 24 hours a day for members

📖

Study Room Booking

Reserve a quiet room for group projects, revision or reading. Rooms have tables, chairs and power sockets.

How to use it

  • Visit the front desk with your membership card.
  • Choose a time slot of up to 2 hours.
  • Arrive on time, since late bookings may be given to others.

Timings: Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM

🖨️

Printing and Scanning

Print assignments or scan documents and book pages at the digital services counter for a small fee.

How to use it

  • Bring your file on a USB drive, or email it to yourself.
  • Ask the counter staff to print or scan it.
  • Pay the small fee at the counter.

Timings: Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM

📶

Free Wi-Fi

Stay connected while you study. Free wireless internet is available in every reading area of the library.

How to use it

  • Turn on Wi-Fi on your device.
  • Select the "ReadersHaven-Free" network.
  • Open your browser and accept the terms.

Available: during all opening hours

Need help with a service?

Our staff are happy to guide you. Visit the front desk or send us a message.
