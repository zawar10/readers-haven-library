Membership Rules & Regulations

  • Books must be returned on or before the due date.
  • A late fee of PKR 20 per book per day applies after the due date.
  • Members can renew a book once if it has not been reserved by another member.
  • Membership fees are paid annually.
  • Members must present their library membership card when borrowing books.
